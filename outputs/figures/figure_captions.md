# Descriptive figure captions

**Figure 1. Fleet use over time, Norfolk Lime, June 2021 to October 2026 (4,423,221 trips).** (a) Weekly trips by vehicle (stacked). (b) Weekly e-bike share (filled) and the citywide e-bike share of the previous seven days (dashed), used as a strictly lagged proxy for the deployed fleet; bars at the top mark the training, validation and test periods. (c) E-bike share (%) by year and month. (d) E-bike share by start hour (radial length and color). (e) E-bike share by day of week and start hour.

**Figure 2. Trip profile.** (a, b) Ridden distance and duration by year for e-scooters (left half) and e-bikes (right half); 4,000 random trips per vehicle and year, trips of 1-120 min and 1-35 km/h, capped at 8 km and 40 min; horizontal bars are medians and thick ticks the interquartile range. Ridden distance, duration and speed are recorded after the trip and are never model features. (c) E-bike share by straight-line distance between origin and destination tract centroids (the pre-trip distance used in the models) for 2021-2023 and 2024-2026, with Wilson 95% bands. (d) Cumulative distribution of average ridden speed (dotted lines = medians). (e) E-bike share by year for trips within one tract or across tracts and on weekdays or weekends (* = partial year). Steps in (d) reflect the recording precision of distance (0.1 mile) and duration (whole minutes).

**Figure 3. Spatial structure.** (a) Trip origins per km² of land by census tract (log scale). (b) E-bike share by origin tract (tracts with at least 300 trips; gray = fewer). (c) The six spatial blocks used for the spatial transferability tests, defined from tract centroids only (k-means), with each block's share of all trips. (d) The 250 largest flows between different tracts (at least 500 trips; line width ∝ trips, color = e-bike share) and origin volumes (blue dots). (e) Change in e-bike share between 2021-2023 and 2024-2026 (percentage points; tracts with at least 300 trips in each period). Open-water tracts are not shown.

**Figure 4. Weather and neighborhood context.** (a) E-bike share by 3 °C temperature band in dry and rainy hours (marker area ∝ trips; Wilson 95% bands; bands with fewer than 300 trips omitted). (b) E-bike share by temperature and start hour (cells with fewer than 150 trips blank). (c) E-bike share by wind speed in daylight and darkness, 2021-2023 (dashed) and 2024-2026 (solid). (d) E-bike share (%) by quintile of eight origin-tract characteristics (ACS 2023 5-year; LEHD LODES 2022). (e) Tracts with at least 300 trips: distance to downtown against e-bike share, colored by spatial block (marker area ∝ trips); the line joins trip-weighted means of distance octiles.

**Figure 5. Predictive performance.** (a) Validation PR-AUC (2025) of untuned candidates for the main, origin-only and tract-history feature sets; the dashed line is the validation base rate (3.8%). Class weighting destroyed calibration and was rejected. (b) Precision-recall and (c) decile calibration curves of the locked main models on the 2026 test set. (d) PR-AUC divided by the monthly base rate (lift over chance; observed e-bike share printed along the bottom). (e) Mean predicted against observed monthly e-bike share. (f) Test PR-AUC with day-block bootstrap 95% CIs (200 replicates) for all locked models. (g) Optuna search for LightGBM (points = trials, line = best so far). The spline logistic regression is the primary model under the pre-declared rule (validation log loss within 0.001 of LightGBM); decision threshold 0.082 (maximum F1 on validation).

**Figure 6. Spatial transferability.** For each of six spatial blocks, three models with identical settings are compared on trips starting in the block: trained on all areas (seen), on a random subsample of all areas of the same size as the unseen-area training set (size-matched), and on trips that neither start nor end in the block (unseen). (a, b) AUC penalty of not having seen the area (size-matched minus unseen) on 2025 validation trips, by block, for the spline logistic regression and LightGBM. (c) The same penalty with day-block bootstrap 95% CIs (200 replicates). (d, f) AUC by block and condition on 2025 validation and on the 2026 test set; bars span the size-matched to unseen difference. (e, g) Calibration intercept of size-matched (teal) and unseen (red) models; negative values mean e-bike use is over-predicted. Seen and size-matched models perform almost identically, so the penalty reflects spatial novelty rather than the loss of training data.

**Figure 7. What separates e-bike from e-scooter trips.** Explanations on a stratified sample of 117,587 trips from 2026 (up to 1,500 per origin tract), design-weighted to all 2026 trips. (a) SHAP values of the primary spline logistic regression; because the model is additive in log-odds, each value is the feature's centered spline contribution (exact). (b) Mean absolute grouped SHAP value for both models with day-block bootstrap 95% CIs. (c) Mean absolute SHAP value per feature in both models (log scales; color = group). (d) Correlation between the two models' trip-level SHAP values for the 12 most important features of the primary model. Group-level explanations agree; single correlated tract attributes and some time variables do not.

**Figure 8. Effects of the eight most important continuous features.** Black: accumulated local effects (ALE) of LightGBM on the 2026 explanation sample (20 quantile bins; hourly bins for hour). Colored: ALE computed separately on trips starting in each spatial block (B1 to B6). Dashed purple: the spline shape function of the primary model (other features at their medians), centered. All curves are on the log-odds scale. Shape functions of tract attributes in the spline model are irregular because each attribute takes one value per tract and partly acts as a location identifier; group-level summaries are more reliable than single tract attributes. The strips show the design-weighted distribution of each feature; the x-axis spans the 1st to 99th percentile.

**Figure 9. Where explanations and errors cluster.** (a-d) Tract mean SHAP value (log-odds) of four feature groups for the primary spline logistic regression on 2026 trips starting in each tract (red = pushes toward e-bike). (e, f) Local Moran (LISA) clusters (queen contiguity, 999 permutations, p < 0.05) of the LightGBM tract residual (observed minus predicted log-odds on all 2026 trips) and of the LightGBM origin-area contribution. (g) Global Moran's I of tract mean grouped SHAP values for both models, of tract residuals and of the observed e-bike share (* = p < 0.05; ns = not significant). (h) Zones from k-means clustering of standardized tract SHAP profiles (LightGBM, ten most important features; k = 5 chosen by silhouette, maximum 0.19); zones from the primary model's profiles agree moderately (adjusted Rand index 0.34).

**Figure 10. Interactions and zone profiles.** (a) Mean absolute SHAP interaction values of LightGBM (both halves summed) among the ten features with the largest total attribution, 2,000 trips from 2026; main effects account for 49% of total absolute attribution. (b) Mean SHAP value of the ten most important features in each explanation zone (columns: zone, number of tracts, observed 2026 e-bike share). (c-e) SHAP value of a feature against its value, split by a second variable (lines = binned means).
